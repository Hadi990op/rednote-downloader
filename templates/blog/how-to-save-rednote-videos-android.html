How to Download RedNote Videos on Android Phones

Published: August 21, 2026 · Reading time: 5 minutes

Android makes it easy to download RedNote (Xiaohongshu) videos directly to your phone's gallery. Whether you are using a Samsung Galaxy, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Oppo, or any other Android device, the process is the same. No extra apps needed — just your browser and our free tool.

Step 1: Copy the RedNote Video Link

Open the Xiaohongshu app on your Android phone. Find the video you want to save, tap the Share icon, and select Copy Link. The URL is now on your clipboard.

Step 2: Open Our Downloader in Your Browser

Open Chrome, Firefox, or any browser on your Android phone. Go to our RedNote Video Downloader. The page is fully mobile-optimized, so it works great on small screens.

Step 3: Paste and Analyze

Tap the URL input field, long-press, and select Paste. The RedNote link you copied will appear. Tap Analyze to start processing. Within a few seconds, you will see available quality options.

Step 4: Select Quality and Download

Choose your preferred quality — 720p HD for quick downloads, or 1080p/4K for the best quality. Tap the download button. The video will be saved to your phone's Downloads folder.

Step 5: Find the Video in Your Gallery

After the download completes, open your phone's Gallery or Files app. The video will be in the Downloads folder. On most Android phones, downloaded videos automatically appear in the Gallery app within a few seconds.

Device-Specific Tips

Samsung Galaxy

On Samsung devices, downloads go to the My Files app under "Downloads." The video will also appear in the Gallery app automatically.

Xiaomi / MIUI

On Xiaomi devices with MIUI, check the File Manager app. Downloaded videos appear in the "Download" folder.

OnePlus / OxygenOS

On OnePlus devices, use the Files app. Downloads are in the "Downloads" directory by default.

Troubleshooting Android Downloads

Video opens in a new tab instead of downloading

Long-press the download link and select Download link from the context menu. This forces the browser to download the file instead of playing it.

Download fails or shows an error

Make sure the RedNote URL is complete. If you copied a short link (xhslink.com), try opening it in your browser first, then copy the full xiaohongshu.com URL. Check our how-to-download guide for more tips.

Cannot find the downloaded video

Use your phone's file manager to navigate to the Downloads folder. The file will have a .mp4 extension. On some Android versions, you may need to grant the Gallery app permission to access the Downloads folder.

Best Practices for Android Downloads

  • Use Chrome: Chrome handles downloads best on Android. Other browsers work too, but Chrome is the most reliable.
  • Use Wi-Fi for large files: 4K videos can be 100MB+. Downloading on mobile data may be slow and expensive.
  • Clear browser cache: If downloads stop working, clear your browser cache and try again.
  • Check available storage: Before downloading, make sure your phone has enough free storage space.
