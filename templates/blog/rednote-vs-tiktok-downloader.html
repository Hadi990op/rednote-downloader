Video Quality: RedNote Wins with 4K Support

One of the biggest advantages of RedNote over TikTok is video quality. While TikTok caps most videos at 1080p Full HD, RedNote creators frequently upload in 2160p (4K) resolution. This means you can download RedNote videos in stunning 4K quality — something that is simply not possible with most TikTok content.

However, accessing the highest quality RedNote videos (1080p and above) may require authentication cookies. 720p HD quality is available without any login. Check our 4K download guide for details on getting the best quality.

Watermark Removal: RedNote Is Easier

TikTok adds a visible watermark with the creator's username to every downloaded video. Removing this watermark requires a separate tool or a specialized downloader. RedNote, on the other hand, does not add watermarks to its public videos — which means our watermark-free downloader can grab clean videos directly without any extra processing.

Content Types: RedNote Supports More Formats

RedNote is not just a video platform — it supports several content types that TikTok does not:

  • Live Photos: A unique RedNote feature combining a still photo with a short video clip, similar to Apple's Live Photos.
  • Image albums: Multi-image posts with high-resolution photos that can be downloaded individually.
  • Text posts: Articles and text-based content with embedded media.

Which Downloader Should You Use?

If you primarily download RedNote content, our built-in RedNote Video Downloader is your best choice — it handles all content types, supports up to 4K quality, and removes watermarks automatically. For TikTok, you will need a separate tool.

Many content creators use both platforms and often cross-post between them. If that is your workflow, having dedicated downloaders for each platform ensures you always get the best quality from the source.

Conclusion

Both RedNote and TikTok have their strengths. For quality and content variety, RedNote comes out ahead with 4K support and Live Photos. For raw volume and discoverability, TikTok leads. The good news is that whichever platform you prefer, there are free tools available to help you download content for personal use.
