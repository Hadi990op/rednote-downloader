RedNote Video Formats, Resolution & Quality: A Simple Guide

Published: August 21, 2026 · Reading time: 7 minutes

When you download a video from RedNote (Xiaohongshu), the file you receive is typically an MP4 file — but there is more to video quality than just the file extension. Understanding video formats, codecs, resolutions, and bitrates helps you choose the right quality for your needs and ensures you get the best possible download.

What Format Are RedNote Videos?

RedNote videos are served in MP4 format (technically, MP4 is a container, not a codec). Inside the MP4 container, the video and audio are encoded using specific codecs. RedNote primarily uses:

  • Video codec: H.264 (AVC) or H.265 (HEVC)
  • Audio codec: AAC
  • Container: MP4 (.mp4 extension)

H.264 is the most widely compatible codec — it plays on virtually every device and browser. H.265 (HEVC) offers better compression (smaller file sizes for the same quality) but may not play on older devices.

720p HD — The Free Standard

720p (1280x720 pixels) is the most common resolution available without authentication. For most purposes — social media sharing, personal viewing, and archiving — 720p is perfectly adequate. The file size is reasonable, and the quality is sharp enough for phone screens and most monitors.

1080p Full HD — Needs Authentication

1080p (1920x1080 pixels) offers significantly better quality than 720p, with twice the pixel count. However, accessing 1080p streams on RedNote typically requires authentication cookies. This means the server needs to be configured with valid Xiaohongshu login cookies to access higher quality streams. See our HD download guide for more information.

4K (2160p) — The Highest Quality

4K videos have four times the resolution of 1080p (3840x2160 pixels). Not all RedNote content is available in 4K — it depends on what the creator originally uploaded. 4K downloads require authentication and result in large file sizes. Visit our 4K download guide for detailed instructions.

Bitrate: What It Means for Quality

Resolution tells you how many pixels a video has, but bitrate determines how much data is used per second of video. Higher bitrate generally means better quality (less compression artifacts) but larger file sizes.

RedNote's video streams typically have the following bitrates:

  • 720p: ~1500-2500 kbps
  • 1080p: ~3000-5000 kbps
  • 4K: ~8000-15000 kbps

How to Choose the Right Quality

For social media sharing

720p HD is sufficient for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and most social platforms. These platforms re-compress videos anyway, so downloading in 4K for social media is wasteful.

For personal viewing

If you are watching on a phone or tablet, 720p or 1080p is plenty. 4K only makes a visible difference on large screens (55-inch TV or bigger) viewed up close.

For editing or archiving

If you plan to edit the video or want to keep it for future use, always download the highest available quality. You can always compress later, but you cannot add quality that was not downloaded.

For limited data or storage

If you are on mobile data or have limited storage, stick with 720p. The quality is good and the file size is manageable.

Conclusion

Understanding video formats helps you make smart choices about download quality. For most users, 720p HD is the sweet spot — good quality, reasonable file size, and available without authentication. For those who need the best quality and have authentication configured, 1080p and 4K provide stunning detail.
