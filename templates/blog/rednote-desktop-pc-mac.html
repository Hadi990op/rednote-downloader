How to Download RedNote Videos on PC & Mac

Published: August 25, 2026 · Reading time: 5 minutes

Want to save a RedNote (Xiaohongshu) video to your laptop or desktop? You do not need a Windows- or Mac-specific app. Because our RedNote Video Downloader runs in the browser, the same steps work on Windows 10/11, macOS, Linux, and even ChromeOS.

What You Need

  • A computer with any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
  • The share link of the RedNote video (from the app or xiaohongshu.com).
  • An internet connection.

Step-by-Step: Download on Windows

  1. Open the RedNote app or xiaohongshu.com in your browser and find the video.
  2. Tap Share → Copy link.
  3. Open the RedNote Video Downloader in a new tab.
  4. Paste the link into the box and click Get Video.
  5. Pick 720p, 1080p, or 4K, then click Download.
  6. The MP4 saves to your Downloads folder — no watermark.

Step-by-Step: Download on macOS

The steps are identical. On Safari, after clicking Download the file appears in your Downloads stack; on Chrome for Mac it lands in ~/Downloads. For 4K quality see our 4K guide.
