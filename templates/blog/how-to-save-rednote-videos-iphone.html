How to Save RedNote Videos on iPhone (Step-by-Step Guide)

Downloading RedNote (Xiaohongshu) videos on your iPhone or iPad is simpler than you might think. You do not need to install any third-party apps from the App Store or jailbreak your device. All you need is Safari and a few taps. This guide walks you through the entire process.

Step 1: Find the RedNote Video You Want to Download

Open the Xiaohongshu app or website on your iPhone. Browse to the video you want to download. Tap the Share button (usually an arrow icon) on the post, then select Copy Link from the share options. This copies the video's URL to your clipboard.

Step 2: Open the RedNote Video Downloader

Open Safari (or any browser) on your iPhone and navigate to our RedNote Video Downloader. The tool works perfectly on mobile devices — no zooming or scrolling required.

Step 3: Paste the URL and Analyze

Tap on the input field and paste the URL you copied in Step 1. You can do this by tapping and holding the input field, then selecting Paste. Once the URL is in the field, tap the Analyze or Download button.

The tool will process the URL and display available video quality options (e.g., 720p HD, 1080p Full HD, or 4K if available).

Step 4: Choose Quality and Download

Select the video quality you want. For most purposes, 720p HD is sufficient and downloads quickly. If you want the best quality and it is available, choose 1080p or 4K. Tap the download link next to your chosen quality.

Step 5: Save to Your Photo Library

The video will start downloading in Safari. Once the download completes, tap the download icon in Safari's address bar to open the downloaded file. Then tap Save to Photos or Save Video to save it to your iPhone's Photos app.

If the "Save to Photos" option does not appear, tap Share and then select Save Video from the iOS share sheet.

Troubleshooting iPhone Downloads

The video plays instead of downloading

Sometimes Safari plays the video inline instead of downloading it. If this happens, long-press the download link and select Download Linked File from the context menu.

The download is slow

Download speed depends on your internet connection and the video file size. 4K videos can be large (100MB+). If you are on mobile data, try switching to Wi-Fi for faster downloads.

The URL does not work

Make sure you copied the full URL. If you used a short link (xhslink.com), try opening it first to get the full xiaohongshu.com URL. See our detailed download guide for more URL tips.

Tips for iPhone Users

  • Use Safari: Safari has the best download support on iOS. Chrome and Firefox on iOS may handle downloads differently.
  • Check storage space: 4K videos can take up significant space. Make sure you have enough free storage before downloading.
  • Use Wi-Fi: Large video downloads can eat up your mobile data plan quickly.
  • Save to Files app: If you do not want the video in your Photos, you can save it to the Files app instead.
