Step-by-Step: How to Download RedNote Videos

  1. Open RedNote and find the video — Open the RedNote app on your phone or go to xiaohongshu.com in your browser. Browse or search for the video you want to download.
  2. Copy the video link — Tap the Share button on the video, then select Copy Link. The link will be copied to your clipboard. The link will look like https://www.xiaohongshu.com/explore/... or https://xhslink.com/a/...
  3. Open the RedNote Video Downloader — Go to the RedNote Video Downloader in your web browser.
  4. Paste the link — Paste the copied link into the input box on the downloader page.
  5. Click "Get Video" — The tool will fetch all available quality options for the video. This usually takes a few seconds.
  6. Select your preferred quality — Choose from the available options: 720p HD, 1080p Full HD, or 2160p 4K. HD is available without login. 4K requires cookie configuration.
  7. Click "Download" — The video will be downloaded to your device. The file is watermark-free and in the quality you selected.
