DMCA / Copyright Takedown Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

RedNote Video Downloader respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users to do the same. This DMCA policy explains how to report content that you believe infringes your copyright.

How Our Service Works

RedNote Video Downloader is a real-time video extraction tool. When a user pastes a public Xiaohongshu URL, our tool fetches the video data on-the-fly and provides a temporary download link. We do not host, store, or cache video content on our servers long-term. Downloaded files are automatically deleted within minutes.

Because we do not store content, we cannot remove specific videos from our servers — they are not there. However, we can take action to prevent the Service from being used to download specific Xiaohongshu posts if you hold the copyright.

Filing a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you believe your copyrighted work has been downloaded through our Service without authorization, please send a DMCA notice including the following information:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work: A description of the original work that you claim has been infringed, including the Xiaohongshu post URL.
  2. Identification of the infringing material: The specific Xiaohongshu URL that was downloaded through our Service.
  3. Your contact information: Your full name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  4. Statement of good faith: A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. Statement of accuracy: A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the copyright owner's behalf.
  6. Physical or electronic signature: Your signature or the signature of a person authorized to act on your behalf.

Where to Send DMCA Notices

Please submit your DMCA notice through our contact page with the subject line "DMCA Takedown Request."

We will respond to valid DMCA notices within 48-72 hours and will take appropriate action, which may include blocking the specified URL from being processed by our tool.

Counter-Notification

If you believe your content was wrongly identified as infringing, you may file a counter-notification with the same information listed above, plus a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of a federal court. We will process counter-notifications in accordance with the DMCA.

Repeat Infringers

We reserve the right to restrict access to users who repeatedly download copyrighted content without authorization.

Important Note

RedNote Video Downloader is not a hosting platform. We are a tool that facilitates the downloading of publicly available content. The responsibility for ensuring the right to download and use content lies with the user. Please review our Terms of Service for more information.
